# Surface brief

## Scope

Full-screen interactive projection / experience mode.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A living tropical diorama reacts like a shy habitat, never a game screen.

OWN-WORLD: Black projector field; the supplied Coconut Fall habitat as the complete living centre; deep leaf greens, authored rocks and ground cover, small coral mushroom accents, and long soft-edged shadows. No synthetic platform surrounds it.

STORY: A visitor approaches, foliage parts and rustles, butterflies flee in a loose arc, and nearby frogs make gentle escape hops.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A low, centred island floats in wide green space. Palms and large monstera leaves frame an alert frog; butterflies live above the canopy.

FORM: Code-led Three.js scene based on the supplied diorama reference and local GLB assets.

PERFORMANCE: Live art uses the supplied Coconut Fall habitat as the hero GLB, backed by four cloned supporting plant models, three Frog Pack placements, and five animated butterflies from two local models. Pixel ratio and shadow-map size reduce automatically on devices reporting four or fewer hardware threads.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
