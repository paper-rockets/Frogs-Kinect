# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS, and Three.js modules; chosen for a zero-build, easily movable interactive-wall prototype.

## Users

Visitors standing in front of a projected interactive nature wall. They use ordinary body movement or touch-like hand movement to explore it.

## Product Purpose

Create a calm, playful tropical micro-world that visibly responds to a nearby person without requiring menus or instructions.

## Operating Context

A full-screen wall projection. Mouse and touch are the present input; normalized Kinect hand or body coordinates will replace them later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Full-screen WebGL scene; local GLB assets; subtle vegetation response; animals retreat when a person comes close; no debug or control UI; low asset count for smooth playback.

## Evidence on Hand

Local GLB assets in this folder, including tropical plants, coconut palms, an animated butterfly, and a red-eyed tree frog. A supplied diorama reference image establishes the composition and lighting direction.

## Product Principles

- Let the living scene lead; the interface stays invisible.
- Movement should feel gentle and legible from across a room.
- Keep silhouettes large, calm, and readable on a projection.
- Treat proximity as invitation, never as a game mechanic.
